# 03: Clinical schema and the deterministic seed

**What to build:** One command resets the database and rebuilds the full Willowbrook Care dataset: about 1,000 residents across six facilities with conditions, allergies, medication orders and administrations, vitals, assessments, lab results, care plans, incidents, progress notes, appointments, and family contacts, all drawn from the committed clinical vocabulary. Running it twice yields identical data. The resident list from ticket 02 now shows the real population, and every new table is protected by the same scope policies.

## Comments

**2026-09-10, agent.** Implemented and verified against the hosted project.

- **Schema**: `supabase/migrations/20260910120000_clinical_records.sql` adds conditions, allergies, medication orders and administrations, vitals, assessments, lab results, care plans and goals, incidents, progress notes, appointments, and family contacts, each with soft-delete columns and an `updated_at` trigger. `assessment_kinds` is a reference table holding the eight kinds with their due interval and whether every resident is expected to have one (physician visit 60 days, nursing and fall-risk and lab draw 90, podiatry 90, dental and vision 365, wound check 7). Rooms gained a bed count (`capacity`) so occupancy is computable. `lab_results.abnormal` is a generated column. Composite foreign keys keep a child record on the same resident as its parent. `seed_runs` records each reseed's seed number and anchor. `reset_demo_data()` truncates everything in one call and only the service role may execute it. Applied with `pnpm db:push`.
- **Scope**: every clinical table's policies are one expression, `resident_id in (select id from residents)`, which runs under the residents policies (ADR 0003). Nurses may insert and update rows for residents they can see; there is no delete policy anywhere. The policy integration test now covers residents plus all thirteen clinical tables for all three accounts (exact visible counts against the generator), rejects an out-of-scope progress note and vitals insert with 42501, rejects an out-of-scope condition update, and proves the reset function is denied to a signed-in admin. Documented in `docs/database.md`.
- **Generator**: `src/lib/seed/` (`vocabulary`, `organization`, `residents`, `records`, `text`, `random`, `time`, `index`). Names from the pools, ages normal around 85, 150 current residents per facility at 94 percent occupancy, 100 former. Conditions come from care-home prevalence rules over the catalog with exclusive families and diabetic complications gated on diabetes; medication orders are paired with the conditions the catalog says they treat and never conflict with a medication allergy; allergies carry catalog reactions and severities; vitals and lab values are drawn from the catalog's percentile distributions with condition shifts; assessments get plausible last-done dates so about a fifth of residents are overdue for something; former residents' activity stops at their stay end. Everything derives from a seed number and an **anchor** instant (now, rounded to the hour, or `--anchor`), and ids derive from stable keys so links survive reseeds. Build takes about 0.7 seconds.
- **Command**: `pnpm db:seed` (`scripts/seed/reset.ts`) keeps the three logins, calls `reset_demo_data()`, and inserts in 1,000-row chunks six at a time. Full reseed: 57,054 rows in about 15 seconds. Counts: residents 1,000; conditions 4,168; allergies 1,184; medication orders 5,485; administrations 10,650; vitals 12,018; assessments 8,108; lab results 4,354; care plans 665 and goals 1,594; incidents 769; progress notes 5,700; appointments 1,455; family contacts 1,830; organization 895. Every budgeted table is within twenty percent of the spec.
- **Tests**: 53 pass. `src/lib/seed/seed.test.ts` (19) proves determinism, budget adherence, catalog references for every order, condition, and lab result, the allergy rule, the former-resident rule, bed capacity, name pools, age distribution, expected assessment coverage, the overdue share, percentile adherence, the administration window, staff attribution, and scope helpers. The provisional seed and its test are removed.
- **Shared constants** for later tickets: `src/lib/clinical/assessment-kinds.ts`, `vital-ranges.ts` (normal ranges and `outOfRangeReadings`), `lab-tests.ts` (panels with reference ranges), `medication-schedule.ts` (standard dose hours per frequency). Ten nurses are flagged `is_simulated` for ticket 08. No allergy conflicts are manufactured; ticket 09 authors the hero resident's.
- **Verified live**: `pnpm check` green; dev server on port 3005 renders the nurse's and admin's resident lists from the new population, search, and a resident page. Hand-maintained `database.types.ts` updated for every new table, enum, and function.

Notes for later tickets: the seed's "today" is the day it was run, so reseed before recording (or pin `--anchor`) to have appointments today and tomorrow and vitals from this morning. The policy test reads the latest `seed_runs` row to rebuild the exact dataset in the database. A wound check is due only for residents who have had one (`expected_for_everyone = false`); the four kinds everyone gets count as overdue when absent.
